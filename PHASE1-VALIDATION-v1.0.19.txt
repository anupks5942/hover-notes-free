My HoverNotes v1.0.19 — local automated validation

Architecture:
- Stop-time finalization retrieves original YouTube timestamped captions from 00:00 to the frozen Stop boundary.
- Incremental draft is not treated as the complete factual source.
- Transcript timestamps/text are retained while repeated timestamp hyperlink wrappers are stripped from model input.
- Short transcripts use direct synthesis; longer transcripts use cue-bounded map extraction and marker-preserving hierarchical reduction.
- Screenshot-associated draft excerpts are passed only as limited secondary visual context; the original transcript remains authoritative.
- Final Markdown is topic-organized and validated before replacing the saved draft. User edits and the previous draft are protected on failure.

Known limits:
- Direct mode <=18,000 normalized transcript characters.
- Map chunks approximately 10,000 characters, evidence ledger <=36,000 characters, at most five reduction levels.
- Final response cap: 8,192 output tokens.
- Very long lectures may fail safely and retain the incremental draft; 12-hour single-document output is not guaranteed.
